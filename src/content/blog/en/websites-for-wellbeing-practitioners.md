Booking a first appointment with a practitioner is a step. People don't always know what a session is like, what it costs, or whether the approach will suit them. Your website can clear up those doubts before the first call.

## 1. A feel that matches yours

Before the words comes the impression. Colours, photos, the pace of the page: visitors should sense something of your practice. On [SR Bien-être](/en/work/sr-bien-etre/) in Saint-Raphaël, a welcome screen first invites you to breathe before entering: you understand the spirit of the place straight away.

## 2. One page per treatment, with its price

Sophrology, energy healing, lymphatic drainage, children's yoga: each treatment deserves its own page, with:

- **what it is**, in plain words;
- **who it's for** (adults, teenagers, children);
- **what a session is like**, and how long it lasts;
- **the price**.

Showing your prices reassures people: they know what to expect and arrive more relaxed.

## 3. Who you are

In these fields, people choose a person as much as a method. Share your background, your training and what led you to this practice, with a real photo of you and your practice room.

## 4. The questions people don't like to ask

A well-thought-out FAQ does the work of a first conversation: "What happens in a first session?", "Do I need to wear anything special?", "How many sessions should I plan for?", "Do you do home visits?". SR Bien-être answers them before the first appointment.

## 5. Book without waiting

Once the decision is made, it should be easy: an online booking button visible on every page, and a number or WhatsApp link for those who'd rather talk first.

## 6. The right words

Be clear about what you offer, without promising a cure. Depending on your practice, say that it supports, and doesn't replace, medical care. It's more honest, and it builds more trust.

## Beyond sessions

Some practitioners write, run workshops or give talks. The site of [Annick Taquet-Assoignons](/en/work/annick-taquet-assoignons/), a clinical psychologist and author of eleven books, presents her children's picture books sorted by theme and age, her books for professional and family carers, and her upcoming book signings. The website becomes the shop window for everything you do.
