On the French Riviera, the season changes everything: opening hours, customers, the languages spoken at the counter. Your website, meanwhile, often stays the same all year. Here's how to get it ready so summer visitors find you, understand you and come in.

## Visitors search on their phone, outdoors

A tourist knows neither your name nor your address. They search "restaurant near me", "bike hire Saint-Raphaël", "ice cream Fréjus" on their phone, often on a patchy connection. Your site has to be fast and answer the practical questions in seconds: open or not, where, how much, how to book.

## Summer hours, up to date everywhere

Nothing loses a customer faster than wrong opening hours. Before the season:

- update your **summer hours** on your site **and** your Google listing, identically;
- show any **exceptional closing days** and your **opening dates** if you're seasonal;
- at the end of the season, clearly announce your **closure** and **reopening date**, rather than leaving the site silent.

## Speak your visitors' language

Some of your summer customers read English, Italian or German far better than French. A version in their language, even limited to the essentials (menu, hours, booking, directions), changes their decision.

- [Wall Street](/en/work/wall-street-frejus/) in Fréjus has an English version for visitors.
- [Ristorante da Lola](/en/work/ristorante-da-lola/), in a tourist region of Italy, is in Italian, English, French and German.
- [Plan B Concept](/en/work/plan-b-concept/), which represents overseas owners from Saint-Tropez to Monaco, speaks English and French.

Avoid automatic translation buttons: the translations are rough, and Google doesn't see them. A real version per language, with its own address, is understood by visitors **and** search engines. We explain how in [our article on multilingual websites](/en/blog/multilingual-websites/).

## What's on this week, first

In season, things change fast: evenings, gigs, markets, daily specials, town events. Put what's changing at the top of the page. A visitor torn between two places picks the one that makes them want to go, now.

## Book, order, find you: in one tap

A button to book or call, a WhatsApp link, directions to your door: visible on every page, without searching. On a phone, every step you remove counts.

## A pre-summer checklist

1. Summer hours identical on your site and on Google.
2. At least an English version of your practical details.
3. What's on this week, visible on arrival.
4. Booking, calling and directions in one tap.
5. Recent photos, taken in season.
6. A site that's fast on phones: check it with our [quick website check](/en/website-check/).
