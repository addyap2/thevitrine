In the eastern Var, customers looking for a tradesperson rarely type your name. They type their need and their town: "electrician Fréjus", "plumber Saint-Raphaël", "landscaper Roquebrune-sur-Argens". Whoever appears first, with a clear listing and a reassuring website, gets the call. Here's how to make sure it's you.

## Say exactly where you work

Google doesn't guess your area: it reads it. On your site, spell out the towns you really work in: Fréjus, Saint-Raphaël, Puget-sur-Argens, Roquebrune-sur-Argens, Le Muy, Sainte-Maxime, Draguignan… A clear "Areas we cover" page, and your main town in your home page title, beat a list hidden at the bottom of the page.

Beware the opposite excess: a near-identical page for every town looks like filler to Google, and can hurt the whole site. A page per **service**, and a well-described area, are enough.

## Show your work, and say where it is

Photos of your jobs are your best advertising. Caption them with the type of work and the town: "New fuse board in Fréjus", "Timber decking in Saint-Raphaël". Visitors picture their own project, and Google understands where you work.

[EM Concept](/en/work/em-concept/), an electrician and audio-visual integrator in the Var and Alpes-Maritimes, shows its work in a gallery filterable by type of job.

## Your Google listing, with reviews

For a local search, the Google map appears before websites. A complete listing (precise category, area served, job photos, opening hours) and recent reviews make the difference. Ask every happy customer for a review right after the job: that's when they're most willing. Our article on [getting found on Google locally](/en/blog/getting-found-on-google-locally/) covers the settings.

## Overseas owners

Between Fréjus and Sainte-Maxime, some homes belong to owners who live abroad. They're looking for a tradesperson they can trust, often in English, and prefer writing to phoning. [Plan B Concept](/en/work/plan-b-concept/), which represents these owners from Saint-Tropez to Monaco, has a site in English and French and offers contact by WhatsApp.

## Be reachable straight away

A customer with a breakdown won't wait. The call button, WhatsApp link and quote request should be visible on every page of your site, and work in one tap on a phone. EM Concept offers a free visit or quote request in a single step.

## In short

1. The towns you work in, written clearly.
2. A page per service.
3. Job photos captioned with the town.
4. A complete Google listing, and recent reviews.
5. An English version if you work for overseas owners.
6. Call, WhatsApp and quote in one tap.
