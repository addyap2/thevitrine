We say it openly: we build our websites with the help of artificial intelligence. The next question is usually the same: "So AI makes my website?" No. Here's how it actually works.

## What AI changes

AI is a very fast production tool. It helps us:

- **write the code** for a page, an animation or a form, which we then read through and adjust;
- **try out several layouts** before choosing the right one;
- **draft translations**, which we then have checked and corrected;
- **check** a site's accessibility, speed and search-engine basics.

The result is time saved on the repetitive parts, and we put that time back where it matters.

## What doesn't change

**You talk to a person.** You deal directly with whoever is building your site, from the first call to launch. No anonymous platform, no chain of middlemen.

**Decisions stay human.** What your site should say first, what visitors should be able to do in one tap, the tone it should take: all of that comes from a conversation with you, not from a model.

**Every detail is checked by hand.** We test on phones, we read the text, we fix what isn't right. AI suggests; we decide.

**Your content stays yours.** Your words, your photos, the way you talk to your customers: that's what makes a site believable, and you hold the key to it.

## Why your site won't look generic

A site "made by AI" with no human eye on it tends to look like every other one. Ours start from your business.

Compare a few sites in this window: the [Ristorante da Lola](/en/work/ristorante-da-lola/) pizzeria with its ember tones, the [Wall Street](/en/work/wall-street-frejus/) pub and its weekly agenda, [Filton Athletic FC](/en/work/filton-athletic-fc/) and its Concorde story, or [EM Concept](/en/work/em-concept/), where you can switch off the lights in a home cinema. Same tools, very different results, because each site starts from a different story.

## What it means for you

- **More time for the finishing touches**, the ones that separate a decent site from one people enjoy using.
- **Features within easier reach**: several languages, a full menu, polished animation.
- **A direct line** to someone who understands both your trade and the technical side.
